# Code Of Conduct

Everyone taking part in LocalAir is expected to keep this project
useful, respectful, and safe for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful in issues, discussions, and pull requests.
- Focus on technical facts and reproducible evidence.
- Accept constructive feedback and correct mistakes quickly.
- Protect private information and avoid posting sensitive device data.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discrimination.
- Personal attacks or hostile tone meant to intimidate others.
- Publishing private details without consent, including personal contact info.
- Sharing secrets, tokens, Wi-Fi credentials, or live device identifiers.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository spaces, issue trackers, pull requests,
discussion threads, and other official project communication channels.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, open a minimal issue asking me for a private
contact. Do not include sensitive details in public.

For security vulnerabilities, use the process in `SECURITY.md`.

## Enforcement

I may edit, remove, or lock content that violates this policy.
Repeated or severe violations may result in temporary or permanent bans from
project participation.
